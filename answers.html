Should new blown-in go over rodent-soiled insulation?

Ask for a yes or no on the quote. If droppings, urine, or nesting are in the old material, ask whether that material is vacuumed and bagged out before new insulation is blown. A top-off leaves the old layer in place.

Is sealing the holes part of this price?

Ask who closes eaves, vents, and roofline gaps. A shop can remove animals and still leave the same openings. Attic Guard’s San Diego page says canyon-edge houses see more roof-rat pressure. That is a reason to ask, not a promise about your house.

Who pulls the permit?

Ask if this scope needs a City of San Diego permit and who files it. Attic Guard’s own San Diego page says some attic jobs go through Development Services and that they coordinate when a job calls for it. Confirm on your quote.

Are utility rebates included?

Do not treat a rebate as part of the bid until San Diego Gas & Electric confirms the current program. Ask the shop what paperwork they will hand you.

What does this page cost?

Nothing. Call the number on the shop’s own site.
