Pull Then Blow

Before you pay to blow new insulation, ask whether the old material comes out first.

San Diego shops Answers Disclosure

Homeowners in San Diego often get one number for “insulation” that mixes a top-off, a full pull of rodent-soiled material, sanitizing, and sealing the holes rats used. Those are different jobs. A Southern California owner posted a line-item quote that mixed vacuum removal, rodent screens, sanitizing, and new blown-in on the same ticket.

Ask these before you book

  • Is this a top-off over what is already there, or a pull of old, wet, or contaminated insulation?
  • If droppings or nesting are in the attic, is that material bagged out before new insulation goes in?
  • Who seals eaves, vents, and roofline gaps — this crew, or a different company later?
  • Does the written scope name air sealing, sanitizing, and the insulation type (blown-in, batt, or foam)?
  • If the City of San Diego requires a permit for this scope, who pulls it?
  • What is the trip or inspection fee, and does it credit if you hire the same shop?
